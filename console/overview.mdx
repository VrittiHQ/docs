# Vritti Console Overview

Vritti Console is the central web-based management workspace for administrators, HR managers, and department supervisors. It provides complete oversight of workforce presence, leave applications, shift scheduling, device fleet status, and monthly payroll processing.

## Real-Time Presence Monitor

The dashboard home screen displays immediate live statistics for your institution:

- **Present Today**: Total staff marked present via kiosk face recognition or mobile selfie+GPS.
- **On Time vs Late**: Breakdown of employees arriving within shift grace period versus late entries.
- **On Leave**: Employees with approved casual, sick, earned, or statutory leave for the current day.
- **Absent**: Unexcused absences flagged automatically based on shift timing.

## Navigation & Controls

The left sidebar provides direct access to core administrative modules based on your assigned role permissions:

- **Home**: Executive overview cards and live activity feed.
- **Staff**: Employee master list, department hierarchy, and onboarding management.
- **Attendance**: Daily ledger, location geofences, and manual correction tools.
- **Shifts**: Shift templates and staff assignment matrix.
- **Leave**: Approval portal, leave policy configuration, and holiday calendar.
- **Payroll**: Salary structures, monthly payroll runs, and payslip archives.
- **Kiosks**: Registered terminals, status monitors, and activation codes.
- **Settings**: Organization profile, role permissions, and audit logs.
