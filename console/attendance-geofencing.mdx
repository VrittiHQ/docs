# Attendance & Geofencing Management

Vritti tracks employee presence through an event-sourced architecture. Every punch or scan creates an immutable event log, which is aggregated into daily attendance summaries automatically.

## Attendance Verification Modes

Employees can mark attendance through two verified methods:

1. **Biometric Face Recognition (Vritti Lens)**: Automatic hands-free face detection on tablet kiosk terminals.
2. **Selfie + GPS Geofence (Vritti People)**: Verified selfie punch with high-accuracy GPS coordinates on mobile phones.

## Campus Geofencing Setup

Geofencing ensures that mobile attendance punches are valid only within designated physical boundaries of your institution.

### Creating a Location Geofence Zone

1. Navigate to **Attendance > Locations** in Vritti Console.
2. Click **Add Campus Location**.
3. Enter location name (e.g., Main Campus, Satellite Facility).
4. Set the latitude and longitude coordinates, or use the interactive map picker.
5. Define the **Allowed Radius** (recommended: 50 to 100 meters).
6. Click **Save Location**.

<Note>
  If an employee attempts to punch via Vritti People outside the designated geofence radius, the punch will be rejected and logged as an out-of-bounds attempt.
</Note>

## Manual Attendance Corrections

Administrators and HR managers can manually adjust attendance entries when necessary (e.g. forgotten punches or approved field work).

1. Navigate to **Attendance > Daily Ledger**.
2. Select the date and search for the employee.
3. Click **Edit Entry** next to their record.
4. Update status (Present, Half Day, Absent, On Duty) or punch times.
5. Enter a required **Reason for Correction** for audit tracking.
6. Click **Save Correction**.
