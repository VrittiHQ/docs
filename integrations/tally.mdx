# Tally Integration

Virtually every business and institution across India relies on **Tally Prime** (or Tally 9) as their primary accounting software. Vritti provides direct synchronization with Tally to eliminate manual voucher entries and reconciliation errors.

---

## Integration Capabilities

### 1. Automated Attendance-to-Payroll Ledger Sync
Export monthly attendance summaries, loss-of-pay (LOP) days, and calculated gross salaries directly into Tally Payroll Vouchers without manual re-entry.

### 2. Statutory Journal Entry Generation
Automatically create accurate debit and credit vouchers for:
- **EPF Employer Contribution** (3.67% EPF + 8.33% EPS + Administrative Charges)
- **ESI Employer Contribution** (3.25%)
- **Professional Tax (PT)** state liabilities
- **TDS Deductions** under Section 192

### 3. Cost Center & Department Mapping
Map Vritti institutional departments (e.g., Teaching, Admin, Facilities, Security) directly to Tally Cost Centers for departmental P&L tracking.
