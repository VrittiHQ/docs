# Welcome to Vritti

**Vritti** (from *वृत्ति*, meaning livelihood) is the workforce operational intelligence platform built for Indian institutions, schools, colleges, hospitals, factories, and retail enterprises.

It unifies **contactless biometric face recognition**, **offline-first kiosk management**, **GPS geofenced mobile punches**, **22-language voice AI**, **shift scheduling**, **leave approvals**, and **statutory Indian payroll** into a single, automated operational platform.
