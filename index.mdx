# Welcome to Vritti

**Vritti** (from *वृत्ति*, meaning livelihood) is the workforce operational intelligence platform built for Indian institutions, schools, colleges, hospitals, factories, and retail enterprises.

It unifies **contactless biometric face recognition**, **offline-first kiosk management**, **GPS geofenced mobile punches**, **shift scheduling**, **leave approvals**, and **statutory Indian payroll** into a single, automated operational platform.

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Frictionless Biometric Attendance
Vritti Lens recognizes staff members in under 1 second using face verification with liveness detection. No cards, no PINs, and no physical fingerprint sensors that fail when hands are wet or dirty.

### 2. Privacy by Design & DPDP Act Compliance
Vritti does not store raw photos of employees. Face scans are processed on-device, converted into encrypted digital templates, and the raw image is immediately purged. All data is hosted strictly within Indian jurisdiction in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

### 3. Deterministic Statutory Payroll
Automate monthly payroll calculations with pre-configured rules for Employees' Provident Fund (EPF), Employees' State Insurance (ESI), Professional Tax (PT), and Tax Deducted at Source (TDS), eliminating manual spreadsheet math and payroll errors.

### 4. Offline-First Resiliency
No institution loses attendance data because of Wi-Fi downtime. Vritti Lens records attendance offline in an encrypted queue and automatically syncs with the server when connectivity is restored.
