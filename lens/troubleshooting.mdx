# Kiosk Diagnostics & Troubleshooting

This guide provides steps for resolving technical issues encountered while running Vritti Lens kiosks.

## Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue 1: Face Not Recognized

- **Symptom**: Employee stands in front of camera, but screen displays "Face Not Recognized".
- **Possible Causes**:
  - Employee has not completed initial face profile enrollment.
  - Lighting environment is too dark or backlit.
  - Camera lens is smudged or dirty.
- **Resolution**:
  1. Clean the front camera lens with a microfiber cloth.
  2. Verify that staff face vector profile is active in **Vritti Console**.
  3. Re-enroll employee face profile via **Vritti People** or Console if facial appearance has changed significantly.

### Issue 2: Offline Queue Not Flushing

- **Symptom**: Cloud icon remains orange with pending queue count despite Wi-Fi connection.
- **Resolution**:
  1. Tap the top-right gear icon and enter your **Admin Security PIN**.
  2. Check **Network Diagnostic Status**.
  3. Click **Force Sync Now**.
  4. If sync fails, verify that tablet clock time matches server time (NTP time synchronization).

### Issue 3: Camera Stream Black or Frozen

- **Resolution**:
  1. Close the app and re-launch.
  2. Ensure no other application (e.g. tablet device management software) is holding camera access.
  3. Restart the Android tablet.

## Accessing the Admin Diagnostic Menu

To access internal device settings:

1. Tap and hold the top title bar of Vritti Lens for 3 seconds.
2. Enter the **Admin PIN** set during initial pairing.
3. Access diagnostic options: **Re-sync Face Database**, **Test Audio Output**, **Force Sync Queue**, and **Unpair Device**.
