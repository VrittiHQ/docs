# Vritti Lens Hardware & Mounting Guide

Vritti Lens converts off-the-shelf Android tablets into high-speed biometric face recognition attendance terminals.

## Recommended Tablet Specifications

To ensure sub-second face identification and optimal camera field-of-view, select devices meeting these hardware specifications:

| Component | Minimum Specification | Recommended Specification |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **OS** | Android 10 (API 29) | Android 12 or newer |
| **RAM** | 3 GB | 4 GB or higher |
| **Front Camera** | 8 MP with auto-exposure | 12 MP wide-angle |
| **Processor** | Octa-core 2.0 GHz | Octa-core 2.4 GHz (Snapdragon / MediaTek) |
| **Screen Size** | 8.0 inches | 10.1 inches to 10.5 inches |
| **Storage** | 32 GB | 64 GB |

## Wall Mounting & Physical Placement

Proper physical placement prevents glare, awkward user posture, and camera obstruction.

### Recommended Height
- Mount the tablet at a center height of **1.45 meters to 1.55 meters (4.75 to 5.1 feet)** from the floor.
- This height comfortably accommodates standing adults ranging from 1.50m to 1.90m without requiring tilt adjustments.

### Lighting Considerations
- Place the tablet in a well-lit area with even front illumination.
- **Avoid direct backlighting**: Do not position the camera facing direct sunlight, open windows, or strong overhead spotlights facing the lens.
- Ensure ambient light level is above 150 lux for accurate image capture.
