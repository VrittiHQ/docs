# Vritti People Setup & Activation

**Vritti People** is the self-service mobile application for employees. It allows staff members to mark attendance via selfie + GPS geofence, enroll their face vector profile, submit leave applications, and view monthly payslips.

## App Installation

1. Open **Google Play Store** (Android) or **Apple App Store** (iOS) on your smartphone.
2. Search for **Vritti People**.
3. Tap **Install**.

## Account Activation

To log in for the first time:

1. Launch **Vritti People**.
2. Enter your registered mobile number or work email address.
3. Enter the 6-digit **Invite Code** provided by your HR administrator or supervisor.
4. Set a 6-character secure password.
5. Tap **Activate Account**.

## Device Permissions Setup

To ensure attendance verification works smoothly, grant the following permissions when prompted:

- **Location (GPS)**: Set permission to **"While Using the App"** or **"Always Allow"** for accurate geofence verification.
- **Camera**: Required to capture selfie punches and complete face vector enrollment.
- **Notifications**: Required to receive leave approval updates and payslip release alerts.
