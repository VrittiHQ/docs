# Staff Self-Service Features

Vritti People empowers employees to manage their work profile, submit leave applications, and view monthly payslips without contacting HR manually.

## Applying for Leave

1. Open **Vritti People** and tap **Leave**.
2. Tap **Apply for Leave**.
3. Fill in request details:
   - **Leave Type**: Select Casual, Sick, Earned, or Comp-Off.
   - **Duration**: Select Start Date and End Date (or Full Day / Half Day).
   - **Reason**: Enter a short note explaining the absence.
   - **Attachment**: Optional (e.g. medical certificate for sick leave).
4. Tap **Submit Request**.

You can track your application status (Pending, Approved, Rejected) in real time under the **My Requests** tab.

## Accessing Payslips

1. Navigate to **Payslips** in the bottom menu.
2. Select the target month (e.g., July 2026).
3. View your salary summary: Gross Earnings, Deductions (EPF, ESI, PT), and Net Pay.
4. Tap **Download PDF Payslip** to save a copy to your phone or share via email.
