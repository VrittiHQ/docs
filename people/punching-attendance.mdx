# Selfie + GPS Mobile Punch Flow

Employees can mark attendance directly from their smartphone using the selfie punch feature within designated campus geofences.

## How to Mark Mobile Attendance

1. Open **Vritti People** on your smartphone.
2. On the home tab, tap **Punch Attendance**.
3. The app will fetch your real-time GPS coordinates and check if you are within your institution's allowed geofence boundary.
4. If your location is verified:
   - Position your face inside the camera frame.
   - Tap **Capture Selfie & Punch**.
5. Once submitted, the screen displays a green success confirmation screen with timestamp and location details.

## Troubleshooting Punch Failures

- **Failure: Outside Geofence Zone**
  - Make sure you are physically located within campus boundaries.
  - Enable high-accuracy location mode in your phone's GPS settings.
- **Failure: Camera Permission Denied**
  - Go to your smartphone Settings > Apps > Vritti People > Permissions and enable Camera access.
