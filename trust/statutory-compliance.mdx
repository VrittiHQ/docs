# Statutory Compliance Coverage

Vritti automates payroll deductions and calculations in full accordance with Indian labor laws and tax regulations, eliminating manual error during monthly payroll runs.

## Employees' Provident Fund (EPF)

### Standard Deductions
- **Employee Contribution**: 12% of basic wage plus Dearness Allowance (DA).
- **Employer Contribution**: 12% split between Employee Pension Scheme (EPS - 8.33% capped at ₹1,250) and EPF (3.67%).
- **Statutory Ceiling**: Configurable application of the standard ₹15,000 monthly basic wage ceiling.

### Admin Controls
- Enable or disable EPF per employee based on eligibility.
- Automated generation of EPF ECR text files ready for upload to the EPFO portal.

## Employee State Insurance (ESI)

### Applicability & Rates
- Applies to employees with a gross monthly wage of ₹21,000 or lower.
- **Employee Contribution**: 0.75% of gross wages.
- **Employer Contribution**: 3.25% of gross wages.

### Automated Eligibility Rules
If an employee's salary increases above ₹21,000 mid-contribution period, Vritti maintains ESI status until the end of the statutory contribution period as mandated by ESIC regulations.

## Professional Tax (PT)

### State-Specific Tax Slabs
Professional Tax varies by state across India. Vritti supports configurable state PT rules:
- **Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh**, and others.
- Gender-based slab variations (e.g., Maharashtra PT exemptions for female staff earning under specified thresholds).
- February month slab adjustments accounted for automatically.

## Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) & LOP

### Income Tax Deductions
- Support for Old and New Tax Regimes.
- Automated monthly TDS deduction calculation based on declared investments and projected annual earnings.

### Loss of Pay (LOP) Integration
- Direct integration between biometric attendance summaries and salary processing.
- Automated calculation of sandwich leave rules, half-day deductions, and late arrival penalties based on institutional policy.
